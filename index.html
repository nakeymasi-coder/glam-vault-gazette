AI & Business Desk

What This Week's AI News Means for Your Business

The Business Angle · Plain Talk

GPT-6.2 is here in two sizes. Astra for your hardest projects, Sol for everyday work — so match the job to the model: big builds get Astra, quick posts and captions get Sol, and you stop paying frontier effort for small tasks.

Gemini Tasks can run errands. In the US it can phone shops, pay with Google Pay and take jobs by email or text — the kind of follow-up and booking work that eats a morning, handled before you've finished your coffee.

Claude Code chains tools in scripts now. Code Mode links steps together, so a workflow you repeat — prepping a launch, checking a page, moving files — becomes one run instead of ten clicks.

Antigravity reset everyone's quotas. If you build with Google's coding tool, you start this week with a full tank across all models — a good window to knock out the builds you've been saving.
