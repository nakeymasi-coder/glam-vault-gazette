AI & Business News Desk

The AI News That Matters This Week

The Business Angle · Plain Talk

Gemini Tasks is now free — and it can make calls. Google rolled out Gemini Tasks free in the US, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia and India (announced November 17). In the US it can now call businesses, buy through Google Pay and sign in with Password Manager. What this means for your business: stop doing the busywork yourself — let Gemini book the appointment, check the price and update your calendar while you sell.

OpenAI is switching off GPT-5 and o3 on December 11. OpenAI emailed November 24: API access to GPT-5, GPT-5 mini, GPT-5 nano, GPT-5 pro, o3 and o3-pro ends December 11, 2026. What this means for your business: if any tool, prompt or workflow you use still calls those models, move it to GPT-6.2 before December 11 or it stops working.

Google AI Pro is half price for Black Friday. Google’s own Black Friday offer: 50% off Google AI Pro, and the deal ends November 26. What this means for your business: if you’ve been on the fence about Google’s AI tools, the cheapest door in is open through tomorrow — grab it before the price goes back up.

Gemini can now work on your Mac. Google rolled out computer use for the Gemini Mac app on November 19: it can click buttons, type, fill out forms and finish multi-step jobs in a background window while you keep working. What this means for your business: hand off the repetitive clicks — form fills, file moves, site checklists — and keep your hands on the creative work.

ChatGPT Ads changes December 9 — check your settings. OpenAI emailed November 24: starting December 9, ChatGPT Ads settings will allow connections to approved partner apps by default. What this means for your business: if you advertise inside ChatGPT, look at your ad settings before December 9 so nothing connects that you didn’t choose.
