The 5-part formula

A good prompt is like ordering at a restaurant. If you just say "food," you get whatever they bring. Tell them exactly what you want, and that's what comes out.

1. Role: who should the AI be?

Always open with "Act as a…". It puts the AI in the right mindset. Act as a senior web designer who builds apps for beginners.

2. Goal: what are you making?

One clear sentence. Build a booking page for my nail salon.

3. Details: who, what, how it looks

Who it's for, what it needs to do, colors, vibe, must-haves. The more you say, the less it guesses.

4. Format: what you want back

A list? Code? 5 options? Say it. Give me one complete HTML file.

5. Rules: what it must NOT do

This is where most people lose. Don't add extra pages. Don't use stock-photo people. Ask me before changing the layout.

Fix a weak prompt

Weak
Make me a website for my candle business.
Strong
Act as a web designer for small handmade brands. Build a one-page website for my candle business, Velvet Wick. It's for women who love cozy, luxury home scents. Include a hero section, 6 product cards with prices, and an order button. Colors: deep plum and gold. Give me one complete HTML file. Don't add a blog or extra pages.

Why it works: it has a role, one goal, real details, a clear format and a rule. Same AI, totally different result.

Turn your prompt into an app in Base44

Step 1: Plan first

Paste this into Base44 before you build anything:

Act as a patient app builder who explains everything in plain words. I want to build [WHAT YOU'RE BUILDING] for [WHO IT'S FOR]. Before you build, tell me in a short list: what pages it needs, what it needs to save, and anything I need to connect. Don't build yet. Ask me any questions first.

Step 2: Build

Answer its questions, then paste the prompt you made in the builder above.

Step 3: Change one thing at a time

Never ask for 10 fixes at once. One change, check it, then the next one.

Act as a careful app editor. Change ONLY this: [THE ONE THING]. Don't touch anything else on the page. Tell me exactly what you changed.

Step 4: When something breaks

Act as a patient app fixer. This isn't working: [WHAT YOU CLICKED] and [WHAT HAPPENED]. Explain in plain words what's wrong, then fix only that.
